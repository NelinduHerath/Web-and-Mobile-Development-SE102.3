NSBM Green University is a premier, fully government-owned degree-awarding institute in Homagama, Sri Lanka. Situated on a sprawling 26-acre campus, it is South Asia's first green university.
Designed as a fully integrated "university town," it combines world-class academic facilities with modern amenities, including on-campus accommodation, supermarkets, and a state-of-the-art recreation complex.

NSBM Green University, pitipana,Homagama, Sri Lanka.
Operating as a self-financed university,NSBM is renowned for its world-class academic offerings with the intention of providing an opportunity for both national and international students to have a fully-fledged education in Sri Lanka. Our state-of-the-art university offers highly recognized undergraduate and postgraduate programmes offered locally or in affiliation with top-ranked foreign universities under five faculties:1 Business2 Computing3Engineering4 Science5Postgraduate Studies.
