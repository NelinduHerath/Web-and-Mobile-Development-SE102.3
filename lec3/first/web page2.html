Why Trees Are Important

Trees are essential to life on Earth. They sustain the planet by purifying the air, fighting climate change, preventing soil erosion, and providing habitats for wildlife. Additionally, trees directly support human survival by supplying food, timber, and vital ingredients used in modern medicine.

Through photosynthesis, trees absorb carbon dioxide from the air and store it in its wood. Trees and plants will store this carbon dioxide throughout their lives, helping slow the gas’s buildup in our atmosphere that has been rapidly warming our planet.In the U.S.,forests are one of the most promising natural climate solutions—that is actions to protect, better manage or restore nature to store more carbon. Trees are looking out for us, so we have to look out for them. Older,larger trees store a lot more carbon than young trees,so it’s important that in addition to planting new trees, we conserve and protect ancient giants of old growth forests.
